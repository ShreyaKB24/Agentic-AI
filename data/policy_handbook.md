# Student Policy Handbook (Sample) — PGDM Batch 2025-27

## Fee Payment Schedule
Programme fees are paid in instalments. Each instalment has a due date shown in the student's fee record. Payments are accepted through the online student payment portal using net banking, UPI or debit/credit card. A payment receipt is emailed within 24 hours of a successful payment.

## Late Fee Rules
If an instalment is not paid by its due date, a late fee of INR 500 per day applies, up to a maximum of INR 5,000. Late fees are added to the next instalment.

## Payment Portal Failure
If the payment portal fails or is unavailable on the due date, the student may pay on the next working day without any late fee. To get this waiver, the student must raise a support request on the same day, mentioning the failure and attaching a screenshot of the error. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding institute holidays.

## Scholarships
Merit scholarships are reviewed once per term by the Scholarship Committee. Scholarship status can be Applied, Under Review, Approved or Rejected. Approved scholarship amounts are adjusted against the next fee instalment. A student whose application is rejected may appeal once, within 15 days of the decision, through a support request.

## Course and Elective Changes
A student may request a change of elective within the first two weeks of a term. Elective changes are subject to seat availability and approval by the Programme Office. A change of programme (for example from Big Data Analytics to AI & Data Science) is allowed only before the start of Term 2 and requires approval from the Programme Chair. All course and elective change requests require formal submission and staff approval before taking effect.

## Admission and Document Requirements
For confirmation of admission to any PGDM programme, candidates must submit verified copies of: (1) Class 10 and 12 marksheets and passing certificates, (2) Undergraduate degree marksheet and degree certificate / provisional certificate, (3) Valid national management entrance exam scorecard (CAT/XAT/GMAT/MAT), (4) Transfer certificate and Migration certificate, and (5) Government photo ID proof (Aadhaar or Passport). Provisional admission may be granted pending final-year graduation results, provided all required documents are submitted before October 31 of the admission year. If a student withdraws admission prior to commencement of classes, the fee is refunded after a standard processing deduction of INR 1,000.

## Support Requests
Students can raise a support request for fees, scholarships, academics, ID cards, IT access and general queries. Each request receives a ticket ID. The support team responds within 2 working days.

## Updating Personal Details
Students may request changes to their registered phone number, email address or elective. Such changes are applied only after verification and approval by the Student Records Office.
